## Surface textures

All from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com), CC0:
`Tiles040` (floor), `PaintedPlaster017` (walls), `OfficeCeiling001` (ceiling),
`Rubber001` (bench tops), `Metal012` (all dark metal parts).

## Built rather than sourced

The centrifuge, the mugs, the whiteboard handwriting and the labels are built from
Unity primitives (no free centrifuge model had a licence this repository allows). Two candidate models were rejected on licence grounds and are not
in the repository: a vintage book set (CC BY-NC, and made with Autodesk educational
software) and a cup (CC BY-SA, whose share-alike terms would follow any edit we made
to it).

## Sound effects

Every sound effect in `Assets/Audio/SFX` except `alarm_lockdown.wav`, and both music
tracks in `Assets/Audio/Music`, were generated with [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io)
Sound Effects, one prompt per sound, for this project.
